Title I Name, seat and purpose

Article 1

The EPFL Spacecraft Team association (hereinafter “the association”) is a non-profit association established in accordance with Articles 60 et seq. of the Swiss Civil Code.

Article 2

The association has its seat in Lausanne.

Article 3

1 The purpose of the association is to bring its members together to organise space missions as part of interdisciplinary projects, and to promote the space industry at EPFL.

2 The association has no political or religious orientation or affiliation.

Title II Members

Article 4

1 EPFL students may be admitted as members of the association.

2 Provided that the association remains made up mainly of EPFL students, EPFL graduates and staff, students from other schools and, exceptionally, any other person interested in the purposes of the association may also be admitted.

3 The coordinator of the interdisciplinary project must be a member of the association. (A mandatory condition for an EPFL interdisciplinary project recognised by the MAKE committee.)

Article 5

1 The admission of a new member is decided by the committee. A refusal may be appealed to the general assembly.

2 Applications for admission are submitted to the committee in writing.

3 By applying for admission, the applicant accepts the statutes of the association without reservation and undertakes to respect the decisions of the general assembly and of the committee.

4 Applicants for admission are subject to a two-week trial period.

Article 6

1 Membership ends by resignation, by exclusion, or when the conditions for admission are no longer met, in particular when the member is no longer enrolled at EPFL.

2 A member may resign from the association at any time. The resignation is submitted to the committee in writing.

3 On a proposal from the committee, the general assembly may exclude a member who seriously acts against the purposes or interests of the association.

Title III Resources

Article 7

1 The resources of the association consist of any membership fees, income from events organised by the association, grants, sponsorship, donations or bequests, and any other income.

2 The relationship between the student association and any future sponsor or donor must not include the sharing of intellectual property. (If intellectual property is shared with a sponsor or donor, every financial transaction will be subject to value added tax (VAT).) (A mandatory condition for an EPFL interdisciplinary project recognised by the MAKE committee.)

Title IV Intellectual property

Article 8

Members of the association waive their intellectual property rights, whatever their contribution to the association. The intellectual property rights belong to the association. (A mandatory condition for an EPFL interdisciplinary project recognised by the MAKE committee.)

Title V Accounts and balance sheet

Article 9

1 The association keeps accounts and a balance sheet.

2 The treasurer presents the annual accounts and balance sheet to the general assembly, together with the auditors' report.

Title VI Organisation

Article 10

The bodies of the association are the general assembly (hereinafter “GA”), the committee and the auditors.

The general assembly

Article 11

1 The GA brings together the members of the association.

2 The GA is the supreme body of the association. Its duties and powers are all those not assigned to another body, in particular:

  • electing the members of the committee and the auditors;
  • deciding on appeals against the refusal of new members and on the exclusion of members;
  • deciding on the activities of the association in line with its purposes;
  • setting the amount of any membership fee;
  • approving the budget, the annual accounts and balance sheet, and the report of the management committee;
  • setting the maximum amount up to which the committee may commit the association;
  • disposing of the association's assets;
  • amending the statutes;
  • deciding on the dissolution of the association.

Article 12

1 The GA holds an ordinary meeting at least once a year, within three months of the end of the last financial year. It is convened by the committee with thirty days' notice.

2 An extraordinary GA is convened whenever the committee considers it appropriate, or at the request of the auditors or of one fifth of the members of the association.

3 The notice convening the GA states its date, place and agenda.

4 The GA is quorate regardless of the number of members present.

5 The GA is chaired by the president of the association or, where applicable, by the vice-president or another member of the committee.

6 The decisions of the GA are recorded in its minutes.

7 The GA may take decisions by circular resolution, in particular by means of an online voting platform.

Article 13

1 Each member has one vote at the GA.

2 The GA decides by a simple majority of the votes cast. In the event of a tie, the president has the casting vote.

3 The GA elects the members of the committee by an absolute majority of the votes cast in the first round and by a relative majority in the second round.

4 The GA decides on appeals concerning admission and on the exclusion of members by an absolute majority of the votes cast.

5 The GA amends the statutes by a two-thirds majority of the votes cast.

6 The GA decides on the dissolution of the association by a two-thirds majority of the votes cast, at an extraordinary GA convened specially for this purpose and attended by at least half of the members. If this quorum is not reached, a new extraordinary GA is convened within twenty days. It is then quorate regardless of the number of members present.

The committee

Article 14

1 The committee is the executive body of the association. It consists of three to five members, including the president, the vice-president and the treasurer.

2 At least half of the members of the committee must be enrolled at EPFL. (A mandatory condition for an EPFL interdisciplinary project recognised by the MAKE committee.)

3 The members of the committee are elected by the GA from among the members of the association for a renewable term of one year. Where possible, at least one of them must have been a member of the committee during the previous term.

Article 15

The committee has the following duties:

  • administering the association;
  • carrying out the decisions of the GA;
  • leading, coordinating and representing the association;
  • managing the resources and the budget;
  • keeping the cash;
  • keeping the accounts and the balance sheet;
  • ensuring that the association runs properly;
  • safeguarding the interests of the association;
  • reporting on its activities to the general assembly.

Article 16

The association is bound by the joint signature of two persons: the president or the vice-president, together with a second member of the committee.

Article 17

1 The committee meets when convened by the president, as often as the conduct of business requires. It must be convened if at least two members of the committee request it.

2 The committee may only deliberate if the president or the vice-president is present.

3 The decisions of the committee are recorded in its minutes.

4 The committee takes its decisions by an absolute majority of the members present. In the event of a tie, the president has the casting vote.

5 In urgent cases, the committee may take decisions by circular resolution, provided that none of its members objects.

The auditors

Article 18

1 Two auditors are elected by the GA from among the members of the association for a renewable term of one year.

2 The auditors submit to the GA a report on the accounts presented to it. They may at any time check the cash, obtain the books and accounting records, and convene an extraordinary GA.

Title VII Dissolution

Article 19

1 If the association is dissolved, the committee in office is responsible for its liquidation.

2 The available net assets are paid in full to a student association with purposes similar to those of the association, chosen in agreement with EPFL.
